# 📝 Feedback Based Review

**Purpose:** This document tracks the feedback received on earlier work and details how Team Infihhnite addressed each point in Markdown Content V2.0 and our Stage 2 repository setup.

### 1. Readability and Chapter Organization (V1.0 Feedback)

* **Feedback received:** Earlier content felt difficult to read as a single long block.

* **Action Taken in V2.0:** We organized the core module into 11 structured chapters across three sections (`Section 1: Big Data`, `Section 2: Connectionist AI`, and `Section 3: Where Big Data Meets Connectionist AI`) under `Stage 2/Task 1: Markdown Content V 2.0/Module Chapters/`, with clear headings, tables, examples, and learner activities/answers.

### 2. Instruction Compliance and Resubmission Alignment

* **Feedback received:** Earlier submission/instruction-compliance issues resulted in a zero score and highlighted the need to follow all Stage 2 requirements closely.

* **Action Taken in V2.0:** We audited our repository structure, moved historical Stage 1 scope and quality check materials into a Stage 1 folder, removed outdated tracking files from the root layout, updated `README.md`, and aligned our work with Sections 2A–2G in preparation for our complete Stage 2 submission package (`Infihhnite_Stage2_Markdown_V2.zip`).

### 3. Content Accuracy, Terminology, and Formatting Fixes

* **Feedback received:** Review checks identified formatting issues, inconsistent terminology and spelling, mismatched data/batch figures, unverified/misaligned sources, and gaps in beginner explanations and activities.

* **Action Taken in V2.0:** As logged in `Stage 2 Content Review.md`, we fixed broken tables and diagrams, standardized American spelling and unified core terms (`Big Data`, `neuron bias` vs. `unfair bias`, parameters as weights plus biases, and min-max scaling / data-leakage precautions), added learner guidance and worked examples, and aligned inline `[1]` citations with chapter reference lists.
